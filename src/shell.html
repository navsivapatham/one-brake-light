Under the hood

Every vehicle is simulated individually with the Intelligent Driver Model (car-following) and MOBIL (lane changing). Each driver perceives the car ahead as it was one reaction time ago. That lag is what lets a short brake tap grow as it passes back through dense traffic. Without it, a platoon damps almost any disturbance.

The sliders below change the drivers. New arrivals pick up the new values right away. Reaction time is applied to everyone immediately.

      What this is not

      It does not forecast DVP travel times on any specific date. Off-ramp queues backing up from the signals below are outside the model, and there is no weather, incident, or construction layer. Delay from a tap is measured against traffic as it was the moment before the tap, not against a no-tap rerun. In very dense traffic, jams also form on their own, so attributing a jam to the tap is generous.
